About

I find the simple version hiding inside every complicated product.

I'm Dalit Barrett, a product designer with 15+ years leading design for consumer apps, enterprise dashboards, and responsive platforms. I'm drawn to messy, multi-layered product problems, the kind where there's no obvious answer and the real work is figuring out what simple looks like.

I do my best work where product thinking and visual craft overlap, where the right layout or interaction can take something overwhelming and make it feel obvious. Based in Austin. Lately focused on AI products that help people without getting in their way.

Complexity wrangler 🧩 The messier the problem, the more I want in.
Systems thinker 🏗️ I design the component, the pattern, and the rule it lives by.
AI-curious 💡 Designing tools that help without hovering.
Pixel-precise 📐 Yes, I will notice that 1px shift.
Austin, TX 🌮 Fueled by breakfast tacos and optimism.
