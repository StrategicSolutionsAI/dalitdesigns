About

Clarity is the throughline in everything I design.

I'm Dalit Barrett, a senior product designer in Austin with roots in graphic design and over 15 years of experience creating digital products across mobile, SaaS, e-commerce, and consumer platforms.

The goal isn't decoration. It's clarity people can feel.

Background

Drawn to complex problems.

I'm drawn to complex problems — the kinds of products that need structure, restraint, and thoughtful decisions to feel effortless on the surface. My approach combines visual sensitivity with product rigor, helping teams turn complexity into interfaces that feel intuitive, useful, and trusted.

Lately, I'm especially interested in designing tools that make modern life more navigable, including AI-powered experiences that support people without overwhelming them.

How I work

What I bring to a team.

01

Clarity over cleverness

I default to the simplest solution that respects the problem. If a layout, flow, or component feels like it needs explanation, it probably needs redesigning.

02

Systems thinking

Every screen is part of a larger system. I design with reusability, consistency, and scalability in mind — not just what looks right in isolation.

03

Ownership through shipping

I stay close to implementation. I write specs, review builds, and iterate with engineers until the product matches the intent — not just the mock.

Beyond the screen

When I'm not designing.

I'm based in Austin, Texas — originally from New York. When I step away from the screen, you'll find me exploring local restaurants, spending time outdoors, or diving into a good book.

I believe the best design work comes from staying curious about the world beyond pixels — how spaces are organized, how language creates clarity, and how small details shape big experiences.
