Case Study

Vitamin Water

Brand-forward responsive design that balances expressive energy with product clarity across every screen size.

Role
Senior Product Designer
Timeline
4 months
Platform
Responsive Web
Deliverables
UX/UI, Responsive System, Prototypes

The Challenge

Letting the brand lead without losing the product

Vitamin Water's digital presence carried strong brand energy — bold color, expressive typography, and high-impact imagery — but the existing responsive experience let that energy overwhelm the product. Browse flows felt disorienting on smaller screens, and product details were buried beneath visual noise.

Customers arriving from social campaigns or retail touchpoints needed to move quickly from discovery to product information. Instead, they encountered layouts that prioritized spectacle over navigation, with inconsistent hierarchy that shifted unpredictably between breakpoints.

The goal was to create a responsive system where the brand's visual confidence remained front and center while giving users a clear, fast path from browsing to product details — without forcing a choice between personality and usability.

Process

Building a system that flexes without breaking

01
Brand & Product Audit

Mapping the tension between expression and function

Audited the existing responsive experience across devices, cataloging where brand expression helped engagement and where it created friction. Identified key drop-off points in the browse-to-product flow and documented inconsistencies in layout behavior across breakpoints.

02
Responsive Strategy

Designing a fluid layout hierarchy

Developed a responsive grid system that preserved the brand's visual weight at large viewports while gracefully prioritizing product content as screens narrowed. Established clear rules for when imagery could dominate and when content needed to lead.

03
Visual Design

Refining the balance between bold and clear

Created a component library that carried the brand's color energy and typographic personality into structured, scannable layouts. Product cards, navigation elements, and detail screens all inherited brand warmth without competing with the information they presented.

04
Prototyping & Testing

Validating across real devices and contexts

Built interactive prototypes spanning mobile through desktop and tested them with users arriving from common entry points — social ads, search, and retail QR codes. Iterated on touch targets, scroll behavior, and transition pacing based on session recordings and direct feedback.

Solution

A responsive experience with brand confidence

Outcomes

Measurable impact
