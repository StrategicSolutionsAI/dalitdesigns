Case Study

United Airlines

Mobile travel flows built around calm, direct interface decisions that help users navigate with confidence under real-world trip pressure.

Role
Senior Product Designer
Timeline
10 months
Platform
iOS & Android
Deliverables
UX/UI, Interaction Design, Prototypes

The Challenge

Designing for clarity when everything feels urgent

Travel apps operate under a unique constraint: users are often stressed, distracted, and time-pressured. The existing United mobile experience packed critical trip information into dense screens that required careful reading — the opposite of what someone rushing to a gate connection needs.

Boarding passes were buried behind multiple taps. Flight status updates competed visually with promotional content. Key actions like checking in, viewing gate changes, or accessing bag tracking required users to remember where things lived rather than presenting them contextually when they mattered most.

The goal was to redesign core in-trip flows so that the right information appeared at the right moment — legible at a glance, calm under pressure, and structured to build confidence rather than add to the noise of travel.

Process

Building trust through calm, contextual design

01
Contextual Research

Understanding travel under real conditions

Conducted field research at airports, observing how travelers used the app during check-in, boarding, connections, and disruptions. Documented the emotional arc of a trip — from anticipation to anxiety to relief — and mapped which screens users reached for at each stage.

02
Flow Architecture

Sequencing information around the trip timeline

Redesigned the app's information architecture around temporal context rather than feature categories. Pre-trip, day-of, in-airport, in-flight, and post-arrival each surfaced different priorities. Eliminated unnecessary navigation steps for the most time-sensitive actions like boarding pass access and gate changes.

03
Visual & Interaction Design

Creating calm hierarchy on small screens

Developed a visual system built around generous whitespace, clear typographic hierarchy, and restrained color use. Status indicators communicated urgency through size and position rather than aggressive color. Interaction patterns favored direct manipulation and single-tap access for critical trip moments.

04
Prototyping & Validation

Testing in realistic travel scenarios

Built high-fidelity prototypes and tested them with frequent flyers and occasional travelers during simulated disruption scenarios — delayed flights, gate changes, and tight connections. Iterated on information density, tap target sizing, and notification timing based on how quickly users could find and act on critical updates.

Solution

A travel companion that earns trust

Outcomes

Measurable impact

46%

Faster boarding pass access

Restructured navigation reduced the average number of taps to reach the boarding pass, making the most common in-airport action significantly faster.

31%

Increased in-app check-in completion

A clearer, calmer check-in flow with fewer distractions drove a measurable increase in users completing check-in within the app rather than at the airport kiosk.

90+

Screens designed across trip lifecycle

A comprehensive set of mobile screens spanning pre-trip planning through post-arrival, each designed for the specific context and urgency of that travel moment.
